Ready to be part of a global movement that pushes the boundaries? At IxDC, you will have the chance to connect with designers from all corners of the world who are committed to innovating and improving human experiences. By becoming a member, you'll not only engage in a vibrant exchange of ideas but also gain access to invaluable resources, mentorship, and opportunities to collaborate on meaningful projects.

IxDC is built on a foundation of inclusion and continuous growth, offering a platform where your unique voice and perspective can make a real impact. Whether you're looking to deepen your skills, contribute to exciting discussions, or grow into a leadership role in the design world, IxDC provides the support and community to help you achieve those goals.

By joining us, you can learn from industry leaders, share your expertise, and expand your professional network—-all while working toward shaping the next generation of visionary designers. As part of IxDC, you won’t just grow personally and professionally—you'll be helping to drive lasting change in the global design landscape.

Together, we’ll learn, grow, and become the future leaders of design. Take the first step today: [bit.ly/ixdc-join](https://bit.ly/ixdc-join)
