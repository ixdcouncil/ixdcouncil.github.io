Join us for World Interaction Design Day 2024, a global celebration that highlights how interaction design can improve lives through creativity and technology. As a part of this international event, our community is proud to host a series of virtual activities on 26 September and 13 October 2024. You’ll have the chance to connect with other designers during networking sessions, engage in insightful design talks, and take part in hands-on workshops, all aimed at exploring new dimensions in design and technology.

This year’s theme, "Adding Dimensions in Design and Technology," focuses on how innovations like AI, AR/VR, and sustainable design are shaping the future. Whether you're a seasoned interaction designer or from a related field like development, academia, or product design, we invite you to be part of these discussions and collaborative sessions. Don't miss this opportunity to expand your knowledge, connect with peers, and help shape the future of design!
